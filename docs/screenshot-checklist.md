# Screenshot Checklist

Use safe demo data and keep `EMAIL_SEND_MODE=mock` while capturing screenshots.

## Required Screenshots

- Dashboard summary.
- Campaign list.
- Campaign detail.
- Lead upload.
- Draft workflow.
- Mock send panel.
- Email accounts/Gmail OAuth.
- Replies.
- Team decisions.
- No-reply section.
- Follow-up drafts.
- Notifications.
- Activity timeline.
- Production readiness doc.

## Capture Notes

- Avoid showing private lead lists unless approved for the demo.
- Do not capture `.env` files, OAuth secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens, or Supabase service role keys.
- Prefer seeded or internal demo records with non-sensitive contact data.
- Capture at least one full-page view and one focused workflow view for the major sections.
- Confirm labels, statuses, and empty states are readable before taking final screenshots.
